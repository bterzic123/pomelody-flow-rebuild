# Growth Audit — pomelody

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/pomelody-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype only:** https://bterzic123.github.io/pomelody-flow-rebuild/?view=proto
**Polish version:** https://bterzic123.github.io/pomelody-flow-rebuild-pl/

pomelody asks every family for their child's name, year of birth and what they need, then shows everyone the same "Try pomelody for 7 days for free". This audit rebuilds the first launch so each family sees their own child on the screen they pay on, in pomelody's own design and at its published prices.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Expected effect |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Take the sign-in off the first screen | CR +5–10% · ARPU +10–17% |
| 2 | Name the child and their picks in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | Make the loader and the next screen read the answers back | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Put the plans and the trial on the first paywall screen | CR +5–15% · ARPU +10–15% |
| 5 | Ask for notifications once the family is in the app | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for pomelody.

## Where the facts come from

Screens, copy, illustrations, euro prices, the 7-day trial and the 2-day reminder come from the pomelody iOS app as recorded on 12 November 2026. The rating (4.8 from 1.3K) and the quoted review come from the Polish App Store listing. The ads and their dates are from Meta, as seen by AppMagic. Nothing on the page is invented.
